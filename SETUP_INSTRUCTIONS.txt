# Aqua Health Policy App — Setup Instructions
# Takes about 10 minutes. Free forever.

## What you'll need
- A free GitHub account (github.com — sign up if you don't have one)
- The 5 files in this zip

## Step-by-step

### 1. Create a GitHub account (if you don't have one)
- Go to github.com
- Click "Sign up" — use any email address
- Choose the FREE plan

### 2. Create a new repository
- Once logged in, click the green "New" button (top left)
- Repository name: aquahealth-policies  (exactly like this, no spaces)
- Make sure "Public" is selected
- Tick "Add a README file"
- Click "Create repository"

### 3. Upload the app files
- You'll see your new repository page
- Click "Add file" → "Upload files"
- Drag ALL 5 files into the upload area:
    index.html
    manifest.json
    sw.js
    icon-192.png
    icon-512.png
- Scroll down and click "Commit changes"

### 4. Turn on GitHub Pages
- Click "Settings" (tab along the top of your repository)
- In the left sidebar, click "Pages"
- Under "Branch", change the dropdown from "None" to "main"
- Click "Save"
- Wait 2 minutes, then refresh the page
- You'll see a green box that says:
  "Your site is live at https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/aquahealth-policies"

### 5. Share the link with your team
- Copy that link (e.g. https://rachael123.github.io/aquahealth-policies)
- Send it to your team via WhatsApp

## Installing on iPad (you + your team)

Each person does this once on their iPad:

1. Open Safari on the iPad
2. Go to the link above
3. Tap the Share button (box with arrow pointing up)
4. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
5. Tap "Add"

The app icon will appear on the home screen.
Open it once while on Wi-Fi — after that it works with NO internet.

## Updating policies in future

1. Log into github.com
2. Go to your aquahealth-policies repository
3. Click on index.html
4. Click the pencil (edit) icon
5. Make your changes
6. Click "Commit changes"
7. The app updates automatically for everyone within a few minutes
   (they'll need Wi-Fi once to get the update, then it works offline again)
